A Vision for Marathwada's Health

Founded in 2016 by renowned Neurosurgeon Dr. Jeevan Bhawllal Rajput, supported by mentor Dr. Sanjay Vijay Patne, and managed by CEO Dr. P.A. Gadge Patil. Our vision was simple: "To Provide the Best Tertiary Care Medical Treatment in the Maharashtra region."

Today, with 3 modular operation theaters, advanced Cath Labs, and a team of 50+ dedicated physicians and 200+ healthcare professionals, we deliver compassionate, patient-centered healing close to home.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class care through advanced technology, ensuring authenticity, reliability, and approachability in every patient interaction.

Our Vision

To be a leader in superspecialty healthcare, setting the standard for excellence, trust, and patient well-being in the region.
