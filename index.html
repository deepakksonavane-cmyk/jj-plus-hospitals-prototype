A Vision for Marathwada's Health

Founded in 2016 by renowned Neurosurgeon Dr. Jeevan Rajput, JJ Plus Hospitals was built on a single vision: To provide the best tertiary and quaternary care in the Marathwada region.

Today, with 3 modular operation theaters, advanced Cath Labs, and a team of 50+ dedicated physicians, we don’t just treat illnesses—we deliver compassionate, patient-centered healing close to home.

"I believe the future for Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and the broader Marathwada region is a vision of growth and progress. To that end, I will strive to assure a strong, accessible, and world-class healthcare foundation for our community."
- Dr. Jeevan Rajput, CMD & Neurosurgeon
